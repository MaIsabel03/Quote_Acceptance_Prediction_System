# Quote Acceptance Prediction System

## Project Overview

The Quote Acceptance Prediction System (QAPS) is a proposed system for a fictional insurance company, Caribe Insurance Group. The system is designed to use historical quote and policy data to predict whether a customer is likely to accept or decline an insurance quote.

The goal is to provide Insurance Agents with predictive insights that can help them prioritize customer follow-ups, improve operational efficiency, and support more informed decision-making.

This project focuses on the **analysis and design of the proposed system**, including business requirements, functional and non-functional requirements, process modeling, system modeling, data modeling, and system evaluation.

## Business Problem

Caribe Insurance Group's existing quoting system generates insurance quotes but does not provide Insurance Agents with information about whether customers are likely to accept or decline those quotes.

QAPS was proposed to address this gap by providing predictive information that could help agents prioritize follow-up activities.

## Business Objectives

The proposed system was designed around three primary business objectives:

- Increase the percentage of insurance quotes that convert into active policies by 5–10% within one year.
- Improve Insurance Agent efficiency by reducing unnecessary follow-ups with customers who are highly likely to accept a quote by 15% within six months.
- Support more consistent and informed decision-making by providing predictive insights related to quote acceptance and decline.

## Requirements Analysis

The project defined both functional and non-functional requirements.

### Functional Requirements

Key functional requirements include:

- Generate insurance quotes based on customer-provided information.
- Predict whether a customer is likely to accept or decline a quote.
- Display prediction results within the internal quoting system.
- Restrict prediction results to authorized internal users.
- Store historical quote and policy outcome data for model training and evaluation.

### Non-Functional Requirements

The project also addressed:

- Usability
- Reliability
- Performance
- Security and access control
- Supportability
- Auditing and compliance

For example, the proposed system requires prediction results to be displayed within three seconds for at least 90% of quote requests and requires access to prediction results to be restricted to authorized internal users.

## System Development Approach

A hybrid software development methodology was proposed for QAPS.

Structured planning and documentation would support stable system components, while iterative development practices would allow the machine learning component to be tested and refined over time.

Stakeholder reviews with Insurance Agents would also be used to validate prediction usefulness and ensure that the proposed functionality aligns with business workflows.

## System Considerations

The project evaluated several factors that could affect the proposed system, including:

- Data quality
- Scalability
- Maintainability
- Security
- Legal considerations
- Ethical considerations
- Model performance and monitoring

The analysis recognizes that changing customer behavior and data patterns could affect prediction performance over time, making monitoring, retraining, documentation, and version control important considerations for the proposed system.
